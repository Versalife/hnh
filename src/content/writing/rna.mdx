Translation requires, principally, three actors: the ribosome, mRNA, and tRNA. Since the ribosome is a robozyme, it wouldnt be too far from the truth to claim that to generate a protein, a eukaryotic cell needs three RNA species: rRNA, mRNA, and tRNA. Each species has a rich biography. In what follows, we'll follow the birth of each species from its cognate DNA sequence, it's maturation process .. Along the way, we'll encounter an amazing cast of supporting characters whose importance cannot be overstated.
